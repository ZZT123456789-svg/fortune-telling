The Tarot feature uses the following open resources:

1. **Tarotoo Tarot Dataset** — complete 78-card Rider–Waite–Smith meanings schema.
   Copyright its contributors, licensed under the MIT License.
   Source: <https://github.com/Tarotoo-com/tarotoo-tarot-dataset>

2. **Tarot.js** — referenced for deck, spread and reading data-model concepts.
   Copyright MarketingPipeline contributors, licensed under the MIT License.
   Source: <https://github.com/MarketingPipeline/Tarot.js>

3. **Rider–Waite–Smith card artwork** — locally cached from the public card
   collection maintained by TarotWhisper, whose download manifest identifies the
   Internet Archive public-domain RWS scan as its source.
   The original 1909 artwork by Pamela Colman Smith is in the public domain.
   Individual source URLs are maintained by `scripts/fetch-tarot-assets.py`.

Chinese UI copy, localized concise meanings, interaction design, archive integration,
credit charging and the AI prompt are original adaptations for DaoWen.
